# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (released 1 November 2026)

- Release 1.0.0 (build 16), 1 November 2026: first public release. All 33 simulations and the 2 reference tools are ready: Foundations (9), Supervised Learning (5), Trees & Ensembles (4), Unsupervised Learning (6), Neural Networks (2), Evaluation & Model Selection (5), Reinforcement Learning (1), Modern AI (1), Which Algorithm? Guide and Glossary. The word "preview" is removed from the badge; saved settings from preview builds are reset once.
- Preview build 15: the last four cards; every card of 4MLSim is now ready. Q-Learning in a Gridworld (cliff walk, two exits and a maze; learning rate, discount, exploration, optimistic start, slippery ground; values and policy arrows learned episode by episode, Q values of a clicked cell, reward per episode, the exact optimal policy by value iteration). How LLMs Predict Text (a tiny trigram language model trained on 64 sentences about economics: next-word probabilities, temperature and top-k, generated text coloured by probability, word embeddings from co-occurrence, attention over the prompt, nearest words). Which Algorithm? Guide (four questions give a ranked list of methods with reasons and links, a checklist for any method, and a side-by-side table of every method). Glossary (120 terms with plain definitions and links to the modules, search and A to Z). Q-learning checked against value iteration.
- Preview build 14: Neural & Evaluation batch, five new modules. Neural Networks: Forward Pass & Backpropagation (network diagram with the values of a clicked observation, forward pass in numbers, 0 to 2 hidden layers, ReLU, tanh or sigmoid, learning rate, training epoch by epoch, boundary, training and test loss, gradient size by layer and vanishing gradients). LSTM for Time Series (unemployment, inflation and industrial production; window, units and learning rate; one-month-ahead forecasts against AR and no-change benchmarks on a time-ordered test set; forget, input and output gates, cell and hidden state month by month; the last step in numbers). Hyperparameter Tuning (grid search versus random search for C and gamma of an RBF SVM with 4-fold cross-validation, the full landscape for teaching, best so far, projection on gamma, refit and test once). Data Snooping & Multiple Testing (up to 300 moving-average trading rules on the same 500 days, naive t-test, Bonferroni, White's Reality Check, Hansen's SPA, Model Confidence Set, the winner in the next year). Explainability: Importance, PDP & SHAP (house prices with gradient boosting or linear regression, permutation importance, partial dependence with ICE curves, exact SHAP waterfall and summary). Gradients checked against numerical derivatives; Reality Check and Model Confidence Set checked against the Python package arch; SHAP additivity and the linear-model formula checked.
- Preview build 13: Unsupervised batch, six new modules. K-Means Clustering (assign and update steps one at a time with pseudocode, k-means++ or random starts, standardizing, WCSS by iteration, elbow and silhouette by k, silhouette plot; supermarket customers, card payments, generic shapes). Hierarchical Clustering (merge by merge on the map and in the dendrogram, single, complete, average and Ward linkage with the distance each one uses, cut by slider or by clicking the tree, merge heights and the largest jump). DBSCAN (core, border and noise points, radius and minimum neighbours, click a point for its neighbourhood, clusters grown step by step, k-distance plot to choose the radius, k-means for comparison; unusual card payments flagged as noise). Gaussian Mixtures & EM (E-step and M-step one at a time, ellipses and probability colours, components table, log-likelihood by iteration, BIC by number of components, k-means for comparison; calm and turbulent market regimes in stock and bond returns). Principal Component Analysis (turn your own direction to find the largest variance, variance by direction, scree plot, loadings, the yield curve's level, slope and curvature, rebuilding a month's curve from a few components, scores over time). Curse of Dimensionality (distances bunch together as dimensions grow, nearest neighbours that are not near, the size of a neighbourhood holding 10% of the data, KNN accuracy falling as useless inputs are added). Results checked against scikit-learn and SciPy (k-means, silhouette, DBSCAN, linkage heights, Gaussian mixture EM, PCA).
- Preview build 12: SVM & Trees batch, six new modules. Decision Tree (classification or regression, grown split by split with a stepper, the map with the leaf boxes and the tree diagram, how the question is chosen with Gini, entropy or squared error, the tree as if-then rules, depth and minimum leaf size). Random Forest (one tree of the forest on its bootstrap sample, the vote of all trees for a point, error versus number of trees with the out-of-bag error, feature importance). Gradient Boosting (round by round: residuals, the small tree fitted to them, the learning rate, training and test error by round). XGBoost (gradients and hessians, leaf weights and gains with lambda and gamma, pruning, a numbers card). SVM Linear (separator, margins and support vectors, click to add or delete points, the price of errors C with 4-fold cross-validation, support vector table). SVM Kernel Trick (the lecture's banks with one ratio, debt-to-equity, bad at both ends: linear kernel gives one cutoff, polynomial kernel gives two, shown in the lifted feature space where the separator is a straight line; circles, moons, XOR and banks with linear, polynomial and RBF kernels; which kernel by cross-validation). Results checked against scikit-learn and xgboost.
- Preview build 11: Classification batch, five new modules. Logistic Regression (S-curve, Newton steps to the maximum likelihood with pseudocode, coefficient table with odds ratios as statsmodels Logit, threshold and cutoff, confusion matrix; bank failures, recessions and the yield curve, loan defaults). K-Nearest Neighbors (click a new observation and see its k neighbours vote, Euclidean or Manhattan distance, standardized inputs, accuracy by k with 4-fold cross-validation on the training set). Naive Bayes (priors and bell-curve likelihoods, Bayes' rule step by step for a clicked point, equal or data priors, correlated firm ratios that break the naive assumption, comparison with the logit). Confusion Matrix & Metrics (every test bank on a probability line split into TP, FN, FP, TN; share of failed banks; naive models all healthy / all failed; metrics with the numbers filled in; metrics by threshold). ROC, Precision-Recall & Calibration (ROC and PR curves with AUC and average precision, costs of FN and FP with the lowest-cost T chosen on the training set, reliability diagram and Brier score for logit, naive Bayes and KNN). All results checked against statsmodels and scikit-learn.
